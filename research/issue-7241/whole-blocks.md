# Sharp height bins for every Sobolev exponent on the line

Masullo's Theorems 10.3.1.1(1) and 10.3.2.1(1), pp. 448–455, use finite sections of the spectral quotient built by adding whole zero blocks. At strip width zero there is an unconditional choice of such sections for every real $s>1$ and every number field. It requires no lower bound on the distance between two zeros. [Finite sections in the project](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/8586f1c40558a900ba79fb41487c2567d4bc5268/RiemannMasullo/Ch10_TraceSpectral/Sec3.lean#L405)

Fix a norm splitting and write an idèle class as $\tau(e^x)k$ with $k\in K=C_F^1$. Normalize each character $\omega$ to be trivial on the section. For an arbitrary lift with value $e^{i\beta_\omega x}$ on the section, use the physical ordinate $\nu=t+\beta_\omega$. For almost every $\theta\in[0,1)$, take all sites of a single character with $\nu\in[n+\theta,n+1+\theta)$, retaining every admitted derivative at each site. Write $A_{\omega n}$ for this finite sum of manuscript blocks. For every compact Bruhat test, $\sum_{\omega,n}A_{\omega n}$ converges absolutely in trace norm to its convolution on the spectral quotient. The same full-measure set of shifts works for all tests, all line centers $a$, and every $s>1$ on this field and splitting. [Change of lift for the divisor](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/8586f1c40558a900ba79fb41487c2567d4bc5268/RiemannMasullo/Ch01_Introduction/Sec4.lean#L3517)

## Choosing boundaries

On a fixed elementary stage, $K/U\simeq \mathbb T^d\times A$ with $A$ finite. Write $\ell$ for the integral torus frequency, let $H=1+|\nu|+\|\ell\|_1$, and use the section-normalized Hecke divisors. There is a $D\ge1$ for which the total multiplicity of all sites with $H\le R$ is $O_U((1+R)^D)$. Indeed the angular flow frequencies in the project's joint count are bounded by $C_U\|\ell\|_1$. The count includes all real parts. Dyadic summation gives $\sum_{\omega,\rho}H^{-P}<\infty$ for $P>D$. The measure of shifts within $\epsilon H^{-P}$ modulo one of at least one site is at most $2\epsilon\sum H^{-P}$. Let $\epsilon$ decrease to zero. Almost every shift has a positive $\epsilon_{U,\theta}$ for which every boundary clears every site by at least $\epsilon_{U,\theta}H^{-P}$. The compact group $K$ has countable dual and stage annihilators are finitely generated subgroups of that dual, so only countably many full-measure sets must be intersected. [Joint multiplicity theorem](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/8586f1c40558a900ba79fb41487c2567d4bc5268/RiemannMasullo/AppB_AnalyticNT/Sec4b.lean#L15861) · [Countable angular dual](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/8586f1c40558a900ba79fb41487c2567d4bc5268/RiemannMasullo/Ch10_TraceSpectral/Sec2.lean#L985)

A deterministic alternative chooses a boundary separately for each character and integer $n$. Include the endpoints of $[n+1/4,n+3/4]$ with the $m$ distinct ordinates inside it. The midpoint of a largest gap clears every ordinate, including the exterior ones, by at least $1/[4(m+1)]$. Taking all real parts makes this choice independent of $a$. The joint count bounds $m$ polynomially. Consecutive boundaries are between $1/2$ and $3/2$ apart. The companion Lean file proves a quantitative clearance of the same size by a finite grid argument. [WindowClearance.lean](WindowClearance.lean)

## Why the original complete blocks appear

Use the plus sign for the Fourier transform in $x$. On one angular mode the physical space is $E=L^2((1+x^2)^{s/2}\,dx)$. Write $F(\nu)$ for the transform of an ambient profile and $\lambda_\omega(\nu)$ for the Mellin multiplier of the test. The $m$th jet at a site reads $i^{-m}F^{(m)}(\nu_z)$, with the common Haar normalization. This derivative exists absolutely by Cauchy–Schwarz for every admitted $m$, since $2m+1<s$. The quotient is by the common kernel of these evaluations. The functions $\lambda_\omega$ and every fixed derivative in $\nu$ decay faster than any power jointly in $(\nu,\ell)$ on the test's stage. Differentiation inserts a power of $ix$ on the fixed compact support; integration by parts in $x$ and the torus variables gives the decay. [Actual line jets and common kernel](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/8586f1c40558a900ba79fb41487c2567d4bc5268/RiemannMasullo/Ch10_TraceSpectral/Sec2.lean#L8368) · [Directional Mellin estimates](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/8586f1c40558a900ba79fb41487c2567d4bc5268/RiemannMasullo/AppB_AnalyticNT/Sec4b.lean#L16532)

Put $H_n=1+|n|+\|\ell\|_1$. Smooth the two edges of each bin only inside their empty neighborhoods of radius $c_{U,\theta}H_n^{-P}$, decreasing this radius below $1/8$ when necessary. The resulting compact smooth symbol $\psi$ equals the sharp indicator on a neighborhood of **every** site. Its support has bounded length and its $j$th derivative is $O_j(H_n^{Pj})$. Put $d=\lambda_\omega\psi$. The inverse Fourier transform $\check d$ defines a convolution on $E$. For $w(x)=(1+x^2)^{s/4}$, the elementary inequality $w(x+y)\le 2^{s/4}w(x)w(y)$ and ordinary Young's inequality give

$$
\|C_{\check d}\|_{E\to E}\le 2^{s/4}\int |\check d(y)|w(y)\,dy
\le C_{s,k}(\|d\|_{L^1}+\|d^{(k)}\|_{L^1}),\qquad k>s/2+1 .
$$

The second bound follows by $k$ integrations by parts for $|y|>1$. The right side is $O_L(H_n^{-L})$ for every $L$ by the joint rapid decay, increasing the decay order to absorb $Pk$. Angular projection has norm one. On the selected mode multiplication by $d$ preserves the common jet kernel and therefore descends to the quotient. At every included zero the derivatives of $d F$ agree, through the full retained order, with those of $\lambda_\omega F$. At all other zeros they vanish. By biorthogonality the manuscript's finite sum $A_{\omega n}$ has precisely these responses. The jets separate quotient points, so the two operators coincide. This is where the proof handles the nondiagonal entries at repeated zeros. [Convolution on the actual jets](https://github.com/mccorvie/riemann-masullo/blob/8586f1c40558a900ba79fb41487c2567d4bc5268/RiemannMasullo/Ch10_TraceSpectral/Sec3.lean#L3489)

## Summing the bins

The rank of a bin is bounded by its total retained multiplicity. The sum of ranks for $H_n\le R$ is consequently $O_U((1+R)^D)$. Multiplying the operator estimate by the rank, then summing dyadically, gives

$$
\sum_{H_n>R}\|A_{\omega n}\|_1=O_L((1+R)^{-L}) \qquad \text{for every } L.
$$

The sum agrees with the original convolution on every quotient jet. For any bounded insertion $V$, the grouped traces are absolutely summable by $|\operatorname{Tr}(A_{\omega n}V)|\le\|A_{\omega n}\|_1\|V\|$. Approximation by the terms with $H_n\le R$ gives singular values $O_L(j^{-L})$ by the finite-rank approximation inequality, uniformly also for arbitrary subseries. For a subseries entirely above $R$, combine this with the operator tail to get $O_L((1+R)^{-L/2}j^{-L/2})$. This gives rapid convergence in every Schatten class $S_p$, including $0<p<1$.

Connes used a smooth partition of unity to obtain critical-line trace class and its trace. The earlier project discussion describes the fixed-character smooth-window method and anticipates a choice of gaps. Here the smooth multiplier is constant near every relevant site, precisely realizing a sharp sum of complete manuscript blocks. The joint divisor bound supplies one full-measure set of grids and quantitative convergence across angular modes. Arbitrary enumerations that split the bins, and positive-width strips, remain open. The quantitative clearance lemma is checked in Lean. The operator theorem above is a mathematical proof.
